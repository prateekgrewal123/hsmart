Frequently asked questions

Some common questions about projects, scope, payments and results.

How do I start a project?

Contact HSmart with your requirements. We can discuss scope, deliverables, timeline and pricing before work begins.

How is pricing decided?

Pricing depends on the service, features, complexity, content, integrations and agreed scope. A quote is provided based on the project.

How long does a project take?

Timelines vary according to project size, feedback, content availability and technical requirements.

Can I request revisions?

Revision terms depend on the agreed package or project scope and will be communicated before work begins.

Do you guarantee SEO rankings or advertising results?

No. Search rankings, traffic, leads, advertising costs and conversions can be affected by competition, market conditions, platform policies and other factors outside our control.
