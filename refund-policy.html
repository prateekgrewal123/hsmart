Refund Policy

Refund principles for HSmart digital services and projects.

Digital Services

Because digital work may involve time, planning, development and resource allocation, refund eligibility depends on the stage of work and the agreed project terms.

Before Work Begins

If a client requests cancellation before work has started, any refund will be considered according to the applicable proposal, invoice and payment terms.

Work in Progress

Payments relating to work already performed, resources committed or deliverables completed may be non-refundable to the extent permitted by law and the agreed terms.

Completed Work

Fees for completed and delivered work are generally not refundable unless the applicable agreement states otherwise or a refund is required by law.

Third-Party Costs

Domain, hosting, advertising spend, software licenses, platform fees and other third-party charges may be non-refundable once incurred.

SEO and Marketing

Recurring or ongoing marketing services are subject to the cancellation and billing terms agreed for the relevant service period. Advertising spend paid to platforms is separate from HSmart service fees.

Revisions

A request for changes or dissatisfaction with a subjective design preference does not automatically create a refund right where work was delivered according to the agreed scope.

How to Request a Refund

Send the request with the project/order details and reason through the Contact Us page. HSmart will review it against the applicable agreement and work completed.

Processing

Where a refund is approved, the method and processing time depend on the original payment method and payment provider.

Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as services and payment processes change.
